    The hand-off carries the whole profile, the spec, the physical size, the matplotlib version and the command. It also says what it does not promise: the same style over different data is not the same figure, and a preview here is not evidence that your paper compiles.

    The pages are typesetting fixtures built from each venue’s own style files. Nothing shown here is a paper, a submission or an accepted work, and the prose on the sheet is an excerpt reproduced as typographic context. Style values are the repository’s provisional defaults, not venue requirements.
